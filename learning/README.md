# Learning route

[Project overview](../README.md) · [Scientific route](../science/README.md)

The learning route connects Gaussian optics to a detector that remains calibrated when idler-memory loss and added noise are unknown. It follows one experiment: prepare signal–idler pairs, store the idlers, heterodyne the returns, combine and count all idler outputs, and condition on their total count before making the decision.

The selected educational anchor is Weedbrook and coauthors, [*Gaussian Quantum Information*, Reviews of Modern Physics 84, 621–669 (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.84.621), using the [14 October 2011 manuscript, arXiv:1110.3234v1](https://arxiv.org/html/1110.3234v1). The bridge identifies the precise source sections and equations and translates their quadrature conventions into the project's photon occupations.

The bridge distinguishes the ideal collective rate factor above $`2.2`$, the finite-control exact-decision factor above $`2.17`$, and the repeated fixed-width factor above $`1.05`$. Each compares with phase-informed coherent mixtures allowing arbitrary global reception at the same mean transmitted signal energy. Additional source-idler, calibration, storage, pump, measurement and processing resources retain their own charges. The learning material does not turn these rate bounds into an optimal-receiver or complete-apparatus claim.
